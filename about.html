Meet Our Founder

Joshua Mutsimu

Joshua founded JM Staffing with a vision to revolutionize hospitality staffing in Cape Town. With over 10 years of experience in the events and hospitality industry, Joshua recognized the need for a premium staffing service that combines professionalism with Cape Town's unique warmth and charm.

His hands-on approach and commitment to excellence have built JM Staffing into a trusted name among Cape Town's top event planners, wedding coordinators, and corporate clients. Joshua's leadership ensures that every staff member embodies the company's values of professionalism, reliability, and exceptional service.
